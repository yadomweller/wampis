Story

Sanji is the cook of the Straw Hat Pirates, known for his incredible culinary skills and powerful kicking-based fighting style. He refuses to use his hands in battle to protect them for cooking.

Raised at the Baratie by the chef Zeff, Sanji learned the importance of feeding the hungry. This shaped his strong values and kindness, even toward enemies.

Sanji dreams of finding the All Blue, a legendary sea where fish from all oceans gather. Though he acts cool and composed, he has a soft spot for women and is extremely loyal to his crew.
