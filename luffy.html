Story

Monkey D. Luffy is the captain of the Straw Hat Pirates and a boy with a dream to become the King of the Pirates. After accidentally eating the Gomu Gomu no Mi, his body gained the properties of rubber, allowing him to stretch and fight in unique ways.

Inspired by the pirate Shanks, Luffy set out to sea to find the legendary treasure known as the One Piece. Along his journey, he gathers a loyal crew and faces powerful enemies, all while protecting his friends and following his strong sense of freedom.

Luffy is known for his fearless personality, huge appetite, and unwavering belief in his dreams. No matter how tough the battle is, he never gives up.
