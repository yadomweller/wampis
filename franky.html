Story

Franky is the shipwright of the Straw Hat Pirates and the creator of their ship, the Thousand Sunny. He is a cyborg who modified his own body with powerful weapons and mechanical parts.

Originally known as Cutty Flam, Franky was raised by the legendary shipwright Tom. After a tragic past involving the World Government, he rebuilt himself as a cyborg and became stronger than ever.

Franky is loud, emotional, and proud of his work. Despite his wild personality, he deeply cares for his crew and dreams of building a ship that can sail across the entire world.
