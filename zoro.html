Story

Roronoa Zoro is the swordsman of the Straw Hat Pirates and one of the most powerful fighters in the crew. Known for his unique Three Sword Style, Zoro wields a sword in each hand and one in his mouth.

He joined Luffy early in the journey after being saved from execution. Zoro’s goal is to become the world’s greatest swordsman, a promise he made to his childhood friend Kuina.

Despite his serious and intimidating personality, Zoro is deeply loyal and will risk his life for his captain and crew. He is also famously bad at directions, often getting lost even in simple situations.
