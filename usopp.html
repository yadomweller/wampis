Story

Usopp is the sniper of the Straw Hat Pirates, known for his incredible aim and creative inventions. Though he often appears cowardly, he shows great courage when it truly matters.

Growing up in Syrup Village, Usopp was inspired by his pirate father Yasopp. He used to tell exaggerated stories, many of which oddly became true later on.

Over time, Usopp has grown stronger and more confident, becoming a key member of the crew. His dream is to become a brave warrior of the sea, just like the heroes he admires.
