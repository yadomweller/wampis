Story

Nico Robin is the archaeologist of the Straw Hat Pirates and one of the few people in the world who can read Poneglyphs, ancient stones that reveal the lost history of the world.

As a child from Ohara, Robin was hunted by the World Government after her homeland was destroyed. She spent years on the run, unable to trust anyone, until she met Luffy and his crew.

After being saved by the Straw Hats, Robin finally found a place where she belongs. Calm, intelligent, and sometimes darkly humorous, she now travels with the crew to uncover the true history of the world.
