Story

Brook is the musician of the Straw Hat Pirates and a living skeleton brought back to life by the power of the Yomi Yomi no Mi.

Once a member of the Rumbar Pirates, Brook spent decades alone after his crew perished, keeping a promise to reunite with a whale named Laboon.

Despite his tragic past, Brook is cheerful, loves music, and often makes jokes about his skeleton body. He fights using swordsmanship combined with musical and soul-based abilities.
