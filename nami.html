Story

Nami is the navigator of the Straw Hat Pirates and an expert in reading weather patterns and navigating dangerous seas. She is highly intelligent, resourceful, and has a strong love for money.

As a child, Nami was forced to work for the pirate Arlong, stealing from others to buy back her village’s freedom. After meeting Luffy, she was finally freed from that life and joined the crew.

Nami now travels the Grand Line with her friends, using her skills and her weapon, the Clima-Tact, to control the weather in battle. Her dream is to create a complete map of the world.
