Story

Tony Tony Chopper is the doctor of the Straw Hat Pirates and a reindeer who gained human abilities after eating the Hito Hito no Mi.

Originally rejected by both humans and reindeer, Chopper found a home with Dr. Hiriluk and later Dr. Kureha, who taught him medicine and gave him a purpose in life.

Though he acts cute and shy, Chopper is a skilled doctor and a brave fighter when his friends are in danger. His dream is to become a doctor who can cure any disease.
