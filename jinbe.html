Story

Jinbe is the helmsman of the Straw Hat Pirates and a powerful Fish-Man known for his mastery of Fish-Man Karate. He is a former Warlord of the Sea who chose to follow his own sense of honor and justice.

Jinbe played a major role in protecting Luffy during critical moments, including the Marineford War. His calm personality and wisdom make him one of the most reliable members of the crew.

He believes in equality between humans and Fish-Men and fights to end discrimination. Jinbe officially joined the Straw Hats to help Luffy become the King of the Pirates.
